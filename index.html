ABOUT SEGViC

Moving your goods with confidence.

SEGViC LOGISTICS SERVICES is a Lagos-based logistics and haulage company providing reliable transportation and movement of goods across different locations in Nigeria.

We specialize in heavy-duty truck transportation, upcountry haulage, backloading and corporate transportation solutions for businesses and individuals that require the safe, dependable and efficient movement of goods.
